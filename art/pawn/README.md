# Pawn game mesh

[pawn.mesh](pawn.mesh) is the binary mesh embedded by the Rust game.
The modern soldier pawn holds a forward-facing revolver by its angled grip.
Its cylinder, short barrel, trigger guard, and hammer form a simple silhouette.
The palm and weapon share a joint at the grip so the hand keeps its hold during
animation. The mesh includes surface normals and skinning data for the body,
head, limbs, and weapon. The STL has the same revolver and hand pose.
